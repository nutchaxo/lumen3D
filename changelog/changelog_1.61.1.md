# Changelog v1.61.1 (Web Platform)

## [FIXED]

- **Badge and citation widgets in the light theme**: the default badge pill, the *Copy* button and the BibTeX box of the citation block were painted with `--bg-base`, a token no theme defines, so they fell back to a fixed dark colour and their text was unreadable in the light theme. They now use the page background of the active theme (`--bg-body`); `--bg-base` stays an override that a widget's custom CSS may set. The editor's *Site background* swatch wrote the same undefined token: it now writes `var(--bg-body)`, and colours already stored as `var(--bg-base)` are read as the page background.
- **Call-to-action band**: the subtitle took the grey colour every paragraph gets from `base.css`, unreadable on the coloured band in the light theme; it now follows the band's text colour. With two buttons, the buttons never wrapped and the second one was clipped on a phone; the buttons and their row now shrink and wrap, and a label wraps when a single button is wider than the band.
- **Image alignment and maximum width**: the image widget's *Alignment* had no effect, because `base.css` makes images block-level; the image is now placed by its side margins and follows the same alignment as its caption. A *Maximum width* no longer replaces the column limit (`min(100%, N px)`), so a wide image fits a phone, and it no longer forces the image to the centre.
- **Readable links in text widgets**: links of the rich text, information sheet and icon list widgets used the theme accent, a cyan made for the dark background (1.8:1 on white). In the light theme these widgets now use `--color-accent-text` (`themes.css`: 45 % of the accent over the text colour, `#0E6D8C` for the default accent, 5.9:1 on white; hover 25 %); the dark theme is unchanged. A widget whose custom CSS sets its own `--color-accent` keeps it. The three widgets gain a *Link color* setting.
- **Gallery captions and columns**: with *Zoom on hover*, the rounded clip of each cell cut into the first letter of its caption; only the picture is clipped now. With automatic columns, the gallery chooses its column count from its measured width, so a short last row never holds a single image when another count avoids it (six images on a width that fits five are laid out 3 + 3), and it keeps one row when every image fits.
- **Feature cards of one row**: cards placed side by side, one per column, now take the height of their row (the card's `height: 100%` never resolved), and the card's link sits at the bottom of the card, so the links of a row line up. A column that holds several widgets keeps its layout. The editor shows the same.
- **Pages with workarounds render as before**: pages that compensated for these defects in a widget's custom CSS (`--bg-base: var(--bg-body)` on a citation block, `--text-secondary: …` on a call-to-action band, `--color-accent: …` on a text widget) keep rendering as published. Compared with the previous renderer on the bemine Home and About pages, in both themes, at 375, 900 and 1280 px, the only differences are the fixes themselves: the Home page's card links now sit at the bottom of their cards, its cards share one height at 900 px, and its band subtitle is plain white instead of 88 % white. Test `tests/js/test_page_widgets_theme.mjs`.
