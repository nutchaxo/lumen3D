## [OPTIMIZED]
- **The maximum projection marches at the density the data justifies, and shows at the screen's**: the raw-maximum cache of 1.75.0 is now marched into a target sized to about 2 pixels per voxel footprint (Nyquist) instead of the drawing buffer's, and the recolour pass, which stays at full resolution, interpolates those maxima bilinearly *before* the window and gamma. The overlays, gizmos, labels and every capture keep the screen's resolution (`renderNow()` marches at full resolution; `renderNow({ full: false })` shows what the loop shows). The scale is a function of the camera distance, the data and the screen only — the same while moving and at rest, nothing to do with the interaction. Measured against the full-resolution march on F6TEST-E85 at a 4960×3082 buffer: 46–49 dB at 2 pixels per voxel, 50 dB at half the resolution, 0.003 to 0.2 % of the pixels differing by more than 40 levels (thin-structure silhouettes); interpolating the raw maxima is 1 to 3 dB better than enlarging the coloured image.
- **No change where it cannot help**: at a density under 2 pixels per voxel nothing is reduced (scale 1). On the F6TEST-E85 512 level shown on a tablet-sized screen the specimen covers about 2.3 to 3.2 pixels per voxel, so the gain there is small (15 to 30 % of the march at most); it is larger on a 4K display or when zoomed in. `?renderScale=native` (or `1`) turns it off, `?renderScale=0.5` forces a scale.

## [ADDED]
- **`?perf=1` readout**: a small overlay with the time between rendered frames, the drawing-buffer size and pixel ratio, the render mode, the maxima march's scale, and the volume's path (dense texture, or atlas BC / split / RGBA with or without the 8³ cells). Meant to be read on a device while moving the model, so that a slow frame can be attributed before it is optimised.
- **`VolumeViewer.setRawMarchScale`**, `renderNow({ full })`, and `marchScale` / `autoScale` in `getRawCacheStats()`.

## [NOTE]
- **Smoothness while moving is not solved by this**: the cost of a frame is rays × samples, and a mobile GPU that cannot sustain it at full definition will not become fluid by this change alone. The next step is to read `?perf=1` on the device.
