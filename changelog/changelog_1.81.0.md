## [ADDED]
- **Display sync in Compare**: a new *Display* option (on by default, next to Z-stack / Time / Camera / Channels) keeps how the volumes are drawn the same in every panel. Pressing grid, axes or *hide volume* in one panel does it in the others, and so does the render mode select; a panel that joins later opens in the same state, and what a closed panel shared is forgotten. Photographs have nothing to draw and are left out.
- **`plugin.json#compareSync`**: a toggle plugin that declares `"compareSync": "display"` takes part in that relay through its own `getState()` / `setState()` (`PluginRegistry.getSyncState` / `applySyncState`; `describeToolbar` lists the group). `toggle-grid`, `toggle-axes` and `toggle-volume` declare it (1.0.2). An older copy of these plugins simply stays per panel.
- **`PanelResources` (`js/core/panel-resources.js`)**: one machine budget divided between the panels, pure and tested (`tests/js/test_panel_resources.mjs`).

## [OPTIMIZED]
- **The panels share one GPU budget**: each Compare panel used to believe the whole card was its own, so four panels could ask for four times what the machine holds. The host reads the budget the pages report (`gpuBudgetBytes` in `PANEL_READY`), keeps 70 % of it for all panels, and sends each panel (`PANEL_RESOURCES`) what its atlas needs plus 15 % headroom and an equal part of the rest, which it then plans and evicts its frame cache against. A panel moving between two qualities is given room for both at the swap. The quality plan spends the real budget instead of a fixed 1.5 GiB, and a panel closed returns its room to the others.
- **The panels share the CPU and the network**: the number of volume panels rides on each page's address (`?panels=N`, known before the first brick) and again by message; the decode-worker pool, concurrent decodes, pack bodies in flight (four in all, as one HTTP/1.1 host allows) and the pack / range / prefetch caches are divided by it. Four panels no longer start 32 decode workers and 800 MiB of pack cache. A burst of additions is queued before the first page starts, so every page boots with the right count.
- **No idle polling per panel**: the trust-revocation poll (`api/health` every 8 s, once per panel) only starts when a sandboxed plugin is loaded, on every page; a panel never has one.
- **Renderer switches reach the panels**: `?rawCache=`, `?denseCells=` and `?perf=` given to the Compare page are passed to every panel.

## [FIXED]
- **A Compare panel no longer opens with its sidebar showing**: the sidebar was folded only after the instance config, the translations and the catalog had loaded, so each panel drew it open (320 px, canvas narrower), slid it shut and resized the WebGL canvas while the volume streamed in. It is folded from the first paint (`theme-boot.js` `panel-boot`, `css/viewer.css`).
- **Slice inspector and Z-stack browser fit a panel**: at 300 px they took half of a Compare panel; inside a panel they are now 34 % wide (200–300 px) and the slice keeps the room.
- **Studio no longer stalls the panels' loads**: opening the Compare Studio hides the panels (`display:none`: their pages get no animation frame), so a volume that was still streaming waited, and its 180 s deadline could run out, marking a quality level as failed for good or a panel as not loaded. Deadlines now start over while the Studio is open, no quality step is ordered under it, and the staging resumes when the panels are shown again.
