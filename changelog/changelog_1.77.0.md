## [ADDED]
- **BC7 prototype and its verdict (study A5/M2)**: `js/core/bc7-codec.js` (`BC7Codec`) encodes 3–4 channel bricks as BC7 mode 6 blocks (one line through RGBA space, 1 byte per voxel against 1.5 for BC5 + BC4), with its decoder; `tests/js/test_bc7_codec.mjs` covers the layout and round trip. The decoder was checked against the GPU's own BPTC decoder in the browser: 0 levels of difference over 17 424 texel values. It is not loaded by any page.
- **Measured on 54 real bricks of F6TEST-E85 (native, three channels, the viewer's own uploaded voxels)**: BC5 + BC4 (today) 44.1 dB, BC7 mode 6 31.5 dB; with the display gamma 41.0 against 28.1 dB; over the voxels that hold signal 39.7 against 27.1 dB; encoding 35 ms against 215 ms a brick on the main thread. The channels of these datasets are independent structures, which one line through RGBA space cannot follow. The bar of the study (displayed PSNR ≥ 45 dB) is not reached, even by the current codec at 41 dB on this dataset, so BC7 mode 6 is dropped; a two-subset BC7 mode would still carry three-bit indices per subset and was not attempted.

## [NOTE]
- **A6 (rays bounded by a bounding volume) is not built**: on F6TEST-E85 the 624 occupied bricks of 1922 span the whole volume on every axis, so a bounding box clips nothing: 10.2 against 10.8 ms looking along z and 25.4 against 26.3 ms from the side, the same picture. After the cell skip an empty brick already costs one page-table read, so a per-ray start at the first occupied cell has little left to save here.
