## [ADDED]
- **Graphs plugin (`tools/graphs` 1.0.0)**: a live graph of the dataset on screen. Pick what goes on X and on Y from the variables of the data the page holds, choose how Y is aggregated inside each X bin (mean, median, SD, minimum, maximum, sum, 95th percentile, count, signal volume or area, share above a threshold), optionally draw one curve per category, normalise, switch either axis to log, show the ±SD band, and the plot follows every choice. Two numeric variables can also be drawn as every point or as a density map (co-localisation with Pearson *r* and Manders M1 / M2). *Quick graph* presets start each type from a useful question; the plot and its numbers download as PNG and CSV, and the Download Center takes the plot as a graph export.
- **Variables per dataset type**: *3d / live volumes* — X, Y, Z in µm and as a share of the extent, and the intensity of every channel (channels are matched by name, in their own colour), read lossless through `ctx.data` at the finest level that fits 6 M voxels, cached per mounted timepoint and re-read when the timepoint changes. *2d photographs* — X, Y, red, green, blue, luminance (Rec. 709), saturation and blue excess (B − R, the X-gal stain). *Tracked timelapses* — three tables: cell × frame (time, age, position, speed, distance from start, path, neighbours within the inspector radius, region), one row per cell (lifetime, path, net displacement, straightness, mean and peak speed, division time, daughters, divides, fuses, region) and mean squared displacement per time lag. *Timelapses with signal levels* — the acquisition's signal level and its decay relative to the first frame.
- **Graphs in Compare**: a *Graphs* button and a drawer under the panels. Every open dataset answers the same query from its own panel (`REQUEST_GRAPH_DATA` / `GRAPH_DATA`) and the curves are overlaid, one palette colour per dataset (dash and marker tell several channels of one dataset apart, or the other way round with *Colour by series*), density maps are one per dataset side by side, and a dataset that lacks a variable or fails to answer is named under the plot instead of silently missing. Position variables in % line datasets of different size up.
- **New hosting context `compare`**: a plugin whose `plugin.json#contexts` lists `compare` is loaded by the Compare page itself and receives a host context (`ctx.panels.list/request/onChange`, `ctx.ui.addDrawer`). `compare.html` now loads the plugin registry; a host without plugins is unchanged. `compare.js` answers `GRAPH_DATA` through the shared panel RPC.
- **`DataAccess.levels()`**: the levels of the mounted tree (dimensions and voxel size), so a plugin can choose the level to read.
- **`addCanvasPanel` on the 2D page**: plugins can float a panel over the photograph, as they already could over the volume.

## [CHANGED]
- **Hosting contexts**: the closed vocabulary of `plugin.json#contexts` is now `page`, `panel`, `compare`.
