## [CHANGED]
- **The picture no longer depends on the interaction**: dragging the view used to lower the pixel ratio (down to a quarter) and the sample rate (down to 0.1 sample per voxel) and the picture changed when the button was released. The view now renders at the full pixel ratio and one sample per voxel whether it moves or rests; the adaptive controller (`_adaptive`, `_nextAdaptiveState`) and the reduced step budget during a drag are gone.
- **GPU watchdog guard kept, for safety only**: the step cap per ray (`SETTLED_PIXEL_SAMPLE_BUDGET / pixels`) still stretches the step when a frame is measured past 200 ms, and only then; it is never lowered for smoothness. A very large canvas (4K, about 720 samples per ray) shows the cap as a coarser step on the longest rays.
- **Switching a channel on or off no longer recompiles the shader**: the channel defines now follow the channels the volume *has*, and the shader tests the `enN` uniforms it already read, so a toggle, a solo or a decomposition preview is a uniform write and a redraw instead of a blocking recompile of several hundred milliseconds.

## [OPTIMIZED]
- **Empty-space skipping per 8³ cell instead of per 64³ brick**: a new RGBA8 texture (`cellMap`, one texel per 8³ voxels, about 2 MB for a 1946×1946×72 volume) holds the raw per-channel maximum of every cell, over every voxel a trilinear sample inside the cell can read. The ray-marcher skips a cell when the displayed value of that maximum is at or below what the mode ignores anyway (0 in maximum projection, 0.0025 in Natural Fluorescence, 0.01 in Structure DVR), jumping to the cell exit on the existing jittered lattice. The test uses the live window, gamma, opacity and channel switches, so moving a slider recomputes nothing, and the image is unchanged (the skipped samples would each have added nothing). On a CPU port of the march, 69 % of the samples of a sparse test volume are skipped.
- **Cell maxima computed in the decode worker**: `js/core/cell-max.js` (`CellMax.fromBox`) runs in `brick-decode-worker.js` on the voxels the GPU will read, before the BC encode, and travels with the brick (`row.cellMax`, `encoded.cellMax`); the main-thread fallback computes the same thing. `SVRManager` option `cellSkip` allocates the texture through the atlas GL path, counts it in `atlasBytes` and in the level footprint (`_levelFootprint`), and writes a brick's cells in the same call as its voxels (255, never skipped, when a brick comes without them). A GPU that refuses the texture runs without it.
- **BC-compressed atlases stay exact**: a BC block is fitted to all its sixteen texels, so the cells of a compressed brick are widened to whole 4×4 blocks and raised by a measured margin (`CellMax.BC_MARGIN` = 24 levels; the worst overshoot measured on structured test bricks is 7), never touching an all-zero cell.
- **Picking keeps the brick-level skip**: the depth pick interpolates between the values of consecutive samples, so it does not use the cell test.

## [ADDED]
- **Tests for the new paths**: `tests/js/test_cell_max.mjs` (cell maxima against a brute force, trilinear footprint, BC overshoot), `tests/js/test_cell_skip_parity.mjs` (the march with and without the cell test agrees on 21 600 rays, 3 modes, random windows, gammas, opacities and channel switches), `tests/js/test_svr_cell_texture.mjs` (allocation, budget, writers, uniforms, disposal), worker cases in `test_brick_decode_encode.mjs`, and the extra `CELL_SKIP` shader permutations of `test_v3_render_glsl.mjs`.
