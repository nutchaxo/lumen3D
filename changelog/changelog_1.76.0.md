## [ADDED]
- **View-dependent residency, planning core (not wired into the viewer yet)**: `js/core/view-residency.js` (`ViewResidency`) computes from the real camera which bricks of which level the screen needs: a top-down walk of the level pyramid, each brick tested against the view frustum (the very `projection · view · model · texture→object` matrix the ray-marcher uses) and the clip box, refined only where the pixel footprint demands it (a level is enough when its lateral voxel is at most `footprint / 1.5`), never finer than the quality the user chose (`ceilingLod`), with hysteresis so a zoom hovering on a boundary does not flip a brick between two levels. `ViewResidency.Scheduler` turns successive plans into loads and evictions against a slot budget: nothing is asked twice while resident or pending, the base level stays resident as the fallback that leaves no hole, a coarse brick is kept until the finer ones that replace it have landed, eviction is LRU among what the plan does not need.
- **Tests for the four defects of the former region-of-interest detail**: `tests/js/test_view_residency.mjs` checks that the planned set contains every brick a ray of the real projection crosses (and at most 12 % that none crosses), that no brick is finer than the screen needs (counter of voxels per pixel at zoom 0.6 to 32), that with 512 or 1024 chosen no finer level is ever planned or requested at any zoom, that a zoom out-and-in asks nothing it already holds when the slots hold both views, that there is never a hole while finer bricks are in flight, and that hysteresis cuts level flips from 75 to 3 over 60 views hovering on a boundary.
- **Measured with the fit camera (volume filling the view)**: peak resident memory of a native-quality view, BC atlas, slots of 68³, against the whole native level — F6TEST-E85 1946²×72, 3 channels: 0.24 / 0.54 / 0.17 / 0.07 GiB at ×1 / ×2 / ×4 / ×8 against 0.84; E95 5735²×200, 3 channels: 0.48 / 1.21 / 1.50 / 0.68 GiB against 14.23; E8 3789²×257, 4 channels: 0.80 / 3.62 / 1.80 / 0.60 GiB against 10.54 (2.01 GiB at ×2 with 1.0 voxel per pixel). A plan costs 1 to 13 ms on the main thread.

## [NOTE]
- **What is missing for the viewer to use it**: a level-aware page table and shader lookup, and the scheduler glued to `BrickLoader` and `SVRManager`. The levels of format 5/6 are not nested (1946 → 1024 is a ratio of 1.9), so a brick of a coarse level cannot be found from the page-table cell of the finest level: the page table needs one section per level (a single texture, looked up finest-resident-first). The Z-stack slicer shares that lookup and must change with it. None of this is in this release.
