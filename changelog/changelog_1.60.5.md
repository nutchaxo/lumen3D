# Changelog v1.60.5 (Web Platform)

## [FIXED]

- **Detail zoom picks the level the view needs**: the region-of-interest detail chose its level from the distance to the near face of the volume's box, or the camera's near plane once inside it. The slightest zoom therefore asked for the finest level. It now measures the depth of what is actually seen, the 35 % depth quantile of the resident level's non-empty bricks in view (`SVRRoi.depthQuantile`). A level is taken only when a resident voxel covers more than 2 screen pixels (was 1.5). Once chosen, a level is kept until the view leaves it by a factor of 1.3 either way (`chooseDetailLevel({ current })`), so a small zoom near a switch point no longer swaps atlases.
- **Detail fetches only the bricks that matter**: a round no longer tries to fill the whole detail atlas (up to 768 MiB of VRAM). It fetches only the bricks where the resident level does not resolve the view, i.e. those not deeper than the depth at which a resident voxel covers 2 px (`rankVisible({ maxDepth })`). The count is capped at what covers the viewport two bricks deep, between 48 and 320 (`SVRRoi.wantedBricks`). The detail atlas is capped at 384 MiB.
- **Detail no longer downloads whole packs**: detail batches now ask for the byte ranges of their bricks (`byteRanges`) instead of whole 4×4×4 super-block packs of up to 16 MiB. A pack is still fetched whole when most of it is wanted.
- **Moving the camera no longer restarts the download**: detail is fetched in short batches of at most 24 bricks, most important first. A camera move re-ranks the wanted bricks and the next batch follows the new view, but the running batch is no longer cancelled, so nothing already downloaded is thrown away and no brick is fetched twice.
- **Detail already loaded is reused**: zooming out keeps the detail atlas on screen (finer is never wrong) instead of freeing it, so zooming back in downloads nothing. A level switch parks the previous level's atlas instead of disposing it, and returning to that level picks its bricks up again. Both are still freed with their volume, by *off*, and on a budget shortage.
