# Changelog v1.60.8 (Web Platform)

## [FIXED]

- **Bricked datasets never request slice-stack files**: a timelapse made of bricks has no `preview/slices/` or `medium/slices/` files, yet the viewer could still ask for them, hundreds of 404s per timepoint. Two paths did it: the warm-up of the neighbouring frames fell back to the slice stack whenever the brick manifest had not been read yet (for instance while the *Open the saved view* prompt of a `#state=` link was waiting), and a display load whose brick stream failed (GPU budget, an unreadable manifest, no brick delivered) was retried as slices at 512 and again at 256. On a shared host whose firewall bans an address on request bursts, such a burst can lock the visitor out. Whether a dataset has slices is now decided from its declared volume sources (`VolumeSourceManager.hasSliceStack`: an available `webstack` source), never from whether its manifest has arrived. A bricked dataset warms its neighbouring frames from the brick packs once the manifest is read, and nothing before that. A failed brick stream is reported as it is. `VolumeViewer.loadVolume` and `preloadVolume` refuse a bricked dataset (`no-slice-stack`). Test `tests/js/test_bricked_no_slice_requests.mjs`.
